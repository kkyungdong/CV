## About Me / 소개

**Kim Kyung Dong (김경동, 金鏡東)** is a Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Environmental Studies, Seoul National University (SNU), in the Interdisciplinary Program in Landscape Architecture. His research bridges **Generative AI and Urban Design**, exploring how large language models and reasoning structures (Zero-Shot, Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thought) can be applied to urban planning, landscape architecture, and garden culture.

Prior to his doctoral studies, he served as the CEO of **Urbanstone (어반스톤)** and worked as an urban designer/planner at **Urban Platform (어반플랫폼)**, leading numerous public and private projects in urban regeneration, landscape design, and spatial-environmental strategy across Korea. He is a licensed **Engineer in Landscape Architecture, Natural Environment Restoration, and Urban Planning** (Korea).

**김경동**은 서울대학교 환경대학원 협동과정 조경학과 박사과정생으로, **생성형 AI와 도시설계**의 접점에서 대형 언어모델의 추론 구조(Zero-Shot, Chain-of-Thought, Tree-of-Thought)를 도시설계·조경·정원문화 분야에 응용하는 연구를 수행하고 있습니다. 박사 진학 이전에는 **어반스톤** 대표와 **어반플랫폼** 도시설계·계획 팀원으로서 도시재생, 조경설계, 공간환경전략계획 등 다수의 공공·민간 프로젝트를 이끌었으며, **조경기사·자연생태복원기사·도시계획기사** 자격을 보유하고 있습니다.
